Partnership Opportunities

Every contribution becomes an investment in a transformed life. Partners may choose to support any of the following.

Debt Relief

Pay off the debt that keeps a family in bondage at the brick kiln.

Sewing Machines

Equip a family to start a tailoring or sewing business.

Work Vehicles

Provide a rickshaw or motorcycle for income-generating transport work.

Business Startup Equipment

Fund the tools and stock a family needs to launch their own business.

Education & Schools

Help build schools and provide education for brick kiln children.

Vocational & Skills Training

Train young men and women in a trade that leads to steady income.

Follow-Up & Mentoring

Support the ongoing encouragement and accountability each family receives.

Project Oversight

Help fund verification, monitoring, and responsible stewardship of every gift.

Expansion

Help us reach the six brick kiln communities still waiting for help.

Reporting

Annual Reporting

Each year, our ministry will provide partners with:

  • Financial summaries
  • Project updates
  • The number of families assisted
  • Business progress reports
  • Photographs
  • Testimonies
  • Future ministry goals

This allows every partner to clearly see the tangible impact of their generosity.

Impact

Success Indicators

We will measure the success of this project by:

  • Families released from debt
  • Businesses successfully established
  • Families earning a sustainable income
  • Children benefiting from improved household stability
  • Businesses continuing to operate over time
  • Testimonies of transformed lives

Our goal is lasting transformation — not simply the distribution of resources.

Our Commitment

Every donation is a sacred trust. Our ministry is committed to:

  • Integrity
  • Transparency
  • Faithful stewardship
  • Respect for every family we serve
  • Accountability
  • Compassion
  • Wise management of resources
  • Respect for every donor who partners with this vision
